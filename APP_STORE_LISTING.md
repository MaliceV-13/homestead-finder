## Description
**Plain maps show you where land is. The Getaway Report tells you whether it's worth the drive.**

Tap any spot on the map, a listing, or one of your pins and Homestead Finder builds a **Getaway Report**: one hunting-getaway **fit score (0-100)** with clear pros, cons and red flags.
- Public hunting land nearby (USGS PAD-US)
- Verified game species recorded in the area (iNaturalist research-grade)
- Water: creeks, rivers, lakes, ponds, boat ramps, fishing access and wells
- Road access, including a possible-landlocked warning
- FEMA flood zone, slope and buildability, power lines, hospital distance and noise sources

**Your priorities, your score.** In My Priorities you weight solitude, water, public land, game, flood risk and more, or pick a preset (Balanced, Remote hunter, Family-friendly). The report always shows how the score was calculated. Missing data is marked "unknown" and never guessed.

**Find game where it has actually been seen.** Turn on Wild Game to see photo icons for deer, elk, turkey, waterfowl, upland birds and more, plus sport fish placed on the lake or creek where they were recorded. All icons come from verified observations.

**Shortlist and compare.** Star properties and compare them side by side: fit score, price, acres, water, public land, access, flood zone, power, game and deal score.

**Deal Finder (optional, free RentCast key):** live for-sale pins with price labels. Each listing gets a deal score built from $/acre and $/sqft against local listings, days on market, price cuts and the RentCast value estimate. You can filter by land type: hunting land, farm/ranch, timber, cabin, lot or house.

**A detailed 3D map:** hybrid satellite, 3D terrain, topo, trails and backroads, labels that fill in as you zoom, an Explore fly-around, press-and-hold pins, measuring and drawing tools, and offline pins with GeoJSON/KML export.

*The data is informational and not a survey or appraisal. Always verify access, boundaries and regulations with the managing agency.*

## Features
1. Getaway Report with a weighted fit score and a breakdown of how it was calculated
2. My Priorities sliders and presets
3. Wild Game & Fish layer (verified observations only, no external links)
4. Water access section (OSM, USGS NWIS); water rights shown as "unknown", with a hint to check the state agency
5. Shortlist side-by-side compare, reorderable and exportable
6. Recently viewed (stored on the device only)
7. For Sale + Deal Finder (needs a RentCast key; the button stays hidden without one)
8. Hybrid / 3D terrain / topo / trails & backroads; labels that fill in as you zoom
9. Press-and-hold pins, measuring, drawing, KML/GeoJSON export, offline pins
